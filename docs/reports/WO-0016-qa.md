### Design gaps noticed during scope (to be confirmed or refuted by TLC, then reported as findings)
- G1 `reject` always goes to rework (§4), while §5 says ambiguity via `reject` goes to blocked(awaiting_user).
- G2 A cancelled child sends the parent to rework with no class, so `split` (needs conflict/too_large) cannot re-split, and the remaining children have no disposition (O3-8).
- G3 O3-9 alternative "refused precheck counts" means a refused verb mutates counters, against inv 1 ("refused = unchanged").
- G4 No verb clears the revalidate flag on a re-verify path.
- G5 The follow-up draft from a closed-item reply or flag has no table row.
- G6 A dependency on a cancelled item is never satisfied; a ready dependent leaves only through `revise`/`cancel`.
