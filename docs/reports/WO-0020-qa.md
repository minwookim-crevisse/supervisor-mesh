## 1. What changed in the model

The single source for properties, coverage and bounds is `tests/formal/README.md`. Every action still cites its design row or section.

- **D65:** `reject --class`; ambiguity goes to blocked(awaiting_user).
- **D66:** the cancel of a child or parent cascades to the remaining active children, in the same write (`CascadeWrite`).
- **D67:** a split moves already-cancelled children to `former_children[]`. The O3-8 constant is removed.
- **D68:** environment +1 per passing unblock, and a refused precheck is a guard refusal that writes nothing. The O3-9 constant is removed.
- **D69:** a passing verify clears the revalidate flag.
- **D70 and D72:** new (mesh) rows: follow-up, parent verify pass, parent verify fail. Together with the existing mesh rows there are six (mesh) rows.
- **D71:** the cancelling write moves ready dependents to blocked(awaiting_user) `dependency_cancelled`.
- **D73:** `override` cannot target blocked(limit). TableWalk now has `start` in scope, so blocked(limit) is still reached.
- **D74:** one mesh `seq` (`seq`, `limit_seq`) replaces the clock and the tie/lag option. The ClockTies constant and scenario are removed.
- **D75:** append-only `failures[]`; a split keeps the record. The `SplitFailure` constant is removed.
- **D76:** the verify compare-and-set compares only the attempt-relevant fields: state, attempt id, contract version and submitted commit. Inv 11 is read on those fields.
- **D81** (decided 2026-11-18 from this order's finding, below): `ready`, `reassign` and `unblock` refuse a cancelled dependency, as a guard refusal that writes nothing.
  - Inv 8 is now also an action property, `DependsNotCancelledAtReady`.
  - The standalone (mesh) dependency-cancelled step was removed from the model, because under D81 it can never be enabled. The row's effect happens only inside the cancelling write (D71), and its row witness reads it there.
- **Invariants 13-15 added:**
  - inv 13 = `NoOrphanChildren`;
  - inv 14 = `NoWaitOnCancelled` + `DependsNotCancelledAtReady`;
  - inv 15 = `FlagClearedByReverify`.
- **Scenarios:** five removed (CoreO39Refused, RepliesClockTies, SplitConsumed, SplitStayLinked, SplitUnlink); two added (`Resplit`; `DepsRoutes` for the reassign and unblock paths of D81).

## Modelling assumptions (where the design is ambiguous)

1. `reject` keeps its trigger (an attached reply of any class, or a revalidate flag as `defect`).
2. The D66 and D71 cascades happen in the same write as the cancel. Invariants 13 and 14 need that.
3. Cascade-cancelled children are declared side effects of the cancel rows, not rows of their own (section 1: "`cancelled` is also entered by mesh cascades").
4. Inv 11 "version or attempt mismatch" is read as the attempt-relevant fields of section 8. A reply-side write is not stale.
5. A terminal reply carries its hook event's mesh `seq` (section 9a).
6. Append-only `failures[]` is abstracted to the latest record that routes the current rework; the history is not modelled.
7. Reasons such as `parent_rework`, `parent_cancelled` and `dependency_cancelled` are step labels, not record fields.
8. Resplit uses `MaxCancelled = 2` (a cancelled child plus the sibling its cascade cancels).
9. In `unblock`, D81 applies when the precheck passes. A refused precheck is refused first, and either way nothing is written.
10. **Note for the PM:** under D81 the "(mesh) dependency cancelled" row of section 4 (from `ready`) can occur only inside the cancelling write, never as a separate step. The model treats it as part of D71's cascade. If the design means it to be a separate step, that is a question for the PM, not a model change.
